Today
Get oriented
Watch the Pro video walkthrough before opening the dashboard
30 minutes now saves hours of trial and error. The video covers exact setup, strategy selection, and the What If moment that changes how you see your debt.
Critical
Install the dashboard to your home screen
Follow the install instructions on your welcome page. It takes 30 seconds and gives you a native app experience on any device.
Critical
Week 1
Build your plan
Add every debt — don't skip the small ones
Log all accounts: credit cards, student loans, auto, personal. For each you need: name, balance, APR, minimum payment. Check your card statements for exact APRs.
Critical
Add any 0% promotional APR details
If any debt has a promotional rate, tap Edit and enable the promo toggle. Enter the promo APR, end date, transfer fee %, and post-promo APR. The dashboard handles the rate switch automatically.
Important
Go to Strategy and choose your method
Compare Avalanche, Snowball, and Hybrid side by side. Look at the interest difference — if Avalanche saves you $2,000+ more, that's your answer. If the gap is small, Snowball's quick wins may be worth the marginal cost.
Critical
Set your Monthly Extra Payment and write down your Freedom Date
Be realistic — use an amount you can sustain every month, not your best-case scenario. Write the Freedom Date somewhere visible: phone wallpaper, sticky note, calendar reminder.
Critical
Week 2
Run your scenarios
Use the What If modeler — drag the slider to what you could stretch to
Find the point where Time Saved becomes meaningful to you. For most people $100-$300 extra per month moves the Freedom Date by 1-3 years. That's the number to aim for.
Important
Model your next expected windfall in the Windfall Planner
Tax refund, work bonus, side income — enter your expected amount and see exactly how much time it saves. This makes the decision to put it toward debt much easier than leaving it abstract.
Important
Every Month
Build the habit
Log every payment on payday — takes 30 seconds
Open the dashboard, go to Payment Log, enter the amount and date. Do this every payday. The progress bar moves. The milestones unlock. The habit is what separates people who finish from people who start.
Habit
Update balances monthly and check your Freedom Date
Tap Edit on each debt once a month and update the balance from your latest statement. Watch your Freedom Date move closer. That movement is your motivation engine — protect it.
