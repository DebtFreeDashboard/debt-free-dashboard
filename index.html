FAQ

What exactly do I get when I buy? +
You open the dashboard in any web browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox — free, with no account and no sign-up. The free tier takes you all the way to debt-free. A one-time $24 unlocks the premium planning tools, which you activate right in the app with a license key. No subscription, and it works offline after the first load.
Is my financial data safe? +
Your debt figures stay on your device. Everything is stored locally in your browser — no account, and no server ever receives your balances — so we can't see your numbers. Optional reminders send only due days as numbers.
Does it work on my phone? +
Yes. On Android, get it from Google Play. On iPhone, open mydebtdashboard.com/app/dashboard.html in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app, works offline and can send payment reminders.
What if I want to update my balances over time? +
That's exactly what it's built for. Each month, swipe a payment in This Month to log it, or tap Paid as planned to log them all. When a statement arrives, statement check-in matches the balance exactly. Your whole plan recalculates instantly.
Can it remind me when payments are due? +
Yes, for free. Turn on phone notifications (before each payment, every Monday or on the 1st), or add repeating events to your phone's calendar. The reminder service only gets due days as numbers; your balances never leave your phone. On iPhone, notifications need the Home Screen app.
Do I need to connect my bank? +
No. You type in the balance, rate and minimum for each debt, so it works with any lender, including store cards, medical bills and family loans. There is no login to break and nothing to re-link.
Is there a refund policy? +
Yes. If you're not happy with the dashboard within 30 days of purchase, just email us and we'll refund you in full. No questions asked.
