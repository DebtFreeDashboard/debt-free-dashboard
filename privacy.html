Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

The short version. Your debts, balances, payments and progress are stored on your own device, in your own browser. They are not sent to us, because there is no “us” to send them to — DebtFree Dashboard has no server and no user accounts. We cannot see your numbers, and neither can anyone else unless you choose to share them.

Who we are

DebtFree Dashboard is a debt payoff tool at mydebtdashboard.com, run by an independent developer. Questions about anything on this page: support@mydebtdashboard.com.

Your financial information

Everything you enter into the app — debt names, balances, interest rates, minimum payments, payment history, lump sums, extra payments and milestones — is stored using your browser's local storage, on the device you entered it on.

  • It is never transmitted to us. The app has no backend to transmit it to.
  • We have no way to read it, recover it, or hand it to anyone else.
  • It stays on that device until you clear your browser data or delete it in the app.

The practical consequence: if you clear your browser data, that information is gone, and we cannot restore it. Use Export Backup in Settings to keep your own copy.

Optional Google Drive backup

The app may offer an optional backup to your own Google Drive. If you use it:

  • It only runs when you tap the button. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
  • The backup file is written to your Google Drive, under your own Google account. It does not pass through any server of ours.
  • We request only the drive.file permission, which lets the app see and change only the single backup file it creates. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.
  • You can delete the backup file from your Drive, or revoke the app's access entirely, at any time from your Google Account settings.
  • Your purchase/licence details are deliberately excluded from the backup file.

Payment

Purchases are handled by Gumroad, not by us. Card details are entered on Gumroad's systems and we never see or store them. After purchase you receive a licence key, which is stored locally on your device to unlock premium features.

Analytics

We use analytics to understand how the site and app are used in aggregate — which pages people visit, roughly where in the world they are, what device they use. This is about usage patterns, not about you personally, and it never includes anything you typed into the app.

  • Google Analytics on the website and app — Google's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity in the app, which records anonymised interaction data such as clicks and scrolling — Microsoft's privacy statement.

Your debt figures are not part of what these tools collect. You can block them with any standard browser content blocker without affecting how the app works.

Third-party resources

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and its charting library from the jsDelivr CDN. As with any web request, those providers can see your IP address and browser when the files are fetched. No data you entered is included in those requests.

Cookies

We do not use cookies to identify you or to advertise to you. The analytics providers above may set their own cookies or storage; see their policies, linked above. The app's own storage is not a cookie and is never sent to a server.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: clear site data for mydebtdashboard.com in your browser settings, which removes all app data from that device immediately and permanently.
  • Take your data with you: Settings → Export Backup gives you the whole thing as a JSON file you control.
  • Revoke Drive access: if you connected Google Drive, remove DebtFree Dashboard at your Google Account permissions page.
  • Opt out of analytics: use a content blocker, or your browser's tracking protection.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on request — you already hold all of it.

Children

DebtFree Dashboard is intended for adults managing their own debts and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
