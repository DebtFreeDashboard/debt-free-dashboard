Questions & Answers

What exactly do I get when I buy? +
You download a single HTML file that you open in any web browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox. No installation required. Works on any device. Pro tier also includes a private video link (sent by email) and a PDF checklist.
Is the Pro tier worth $50 more than Starter? +
It depends on how you learn. If you're comfortable experimenting on your own, Starter is excellent. If you want to see exactly how someone set up their plan, chose their strategy, and uses the tool day-to-day — the 30-minute walkthrough alone is worth the difference. The action checklist and priority support are bonuses on top of that.
My data won't be shared anywhere? +
Correct. The dashboard stores everything in your browser's local storage — it never sends data anywhere. There's no server involved at all. We literally cannot see your numbers. You can also run it completely offline after download.
Will it work if I switch phones or get a new computer? +
The file itself is yours permanently — just copy it to any device. Because data is stored locally in each browser, you'd need to re-enter your debts on a new device. We recommend bookmarking it on your primary device and using it consistently there.
What's your refund policy? +
Full refund within 30 days, no questions asked. Just email us. We've built something we're genuinely proud of and confident you'll find valuable — but if it's not for you, we won't make it difficult.
Can I share it with my partner or spouse? +
Yes. One purchase covers your household. The file can be opened on multiple devices and used together to plan your family's debt payoff. We just ask that you don't redistribute or resell it.
