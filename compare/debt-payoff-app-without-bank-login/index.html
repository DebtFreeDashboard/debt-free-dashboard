A debt payoff app that never asks for your bank login

Many money apps start by asking you to connect your bank. You can plan a debt payoff without that: the math only needs a balance, a rate and a minimum payment for each debt.

Facts from each product’s own pagesChecked November 28, 2026

What a payoff plan needs

Three numbers per debt: the balance, the interest rate (APR) and the minimum payment. Add the extra you can put toward debt each month, and the math gives a payoff order, a month-by-month schedule and a debt-free date. None of that needs access to your bank.

Why some apps ask for a bank login

A bank connection lets an app pull balances and payments on its own, so there is less to type. The trade-off is that a third-party service holds a connection to your accounts, and the app can see your transactions. Some people are fine with that; others would rather not share it for a payoff plan.

How DebtFree stays current without one

  • Autopay logged for you: mark a debt as on autopay and its payment is logged on the due day.
  • One tap for the month: “Paid as planned” logs every planned payment at once.
  • Statement check-in: type the balance from a new statement and the app squares up the interest.
  • Fill from a statement: pick screenshots of a card statement and the phone reads the balance, rate and minimum. The images never leave the device.
  • Reminders: an optional nudge before each due date.

Where your numbers are kept

On your phone or computer, in the app’s own storage. There is no account and no server copy of your debts. If you want a backup, it goes to your own Google Drive, and only when you turn that on.

Questions people ask

Is it safe to use a debt app without linking my bank?

Without a bank login there are no bank credentials to share. In DebtFree your debt details stay on your device; nothing about your debts is sent to a server.

How accurate is a plan if I type my own balances?

As accurate as the numbers you enter. Updating each balance from your statement once a month keeps the plan close to what your lender shows.

Does DebtFree work offline?
