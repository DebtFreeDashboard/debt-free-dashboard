How they differ

  • Price. Debt Payoff Planner is free with ads, and its Pro version is a subscription ($6.99 a month or $27.99 a year on the App Store). DebtFree shows no ads, and Premium is one payment of $24.
  • Account. Both work without one. Debt Payoff Planner offers an optional account for backup and other devices; DebtFree has none and offers an optional backup to your own Google Drive.
  • Payoff orders. Both do snowball, avalanche and a custom order. DebtFree adds Optimized, which tests orders and uses the one with the lowest total interest.
  • Tracking. Both have you record payments yourself. DebtFree logs autopay payments for you on their due day, logs a month of planned payments in one tap, and can remind you before each due date.
  • Phones. Debt Payoff Planner is in both app stores. DebtFree is on Google Play; on iPhone it runs from the Home Screen as a web app.

Where Debt Payoff Planner is stronger

It has a native iPhone app in the App Store, years of updates and a large community of users and reviews. Its optional account also carries a plan between devices automatically.

What DebtFree adds

  • 0% promos that end on time: promo and balance-transfer rates switch to the regular rate on the end date.
  • What-If slider: drag an extra monthly amount and watch the debt-free date and interest change.
  • Fill from a statement: the phone reads a card statement’s balance, rate and minimum from screenshots, on the device.
  • Monthly recap and milestones: what changed last month, and the wins along the way.

Questions people ask

Does DebtFree have ads?

No. There are no ads in the free version or in Premium.

Is DebtFree a subscription?

No. The planner is free, and Premium is a one-time $24 purchase with nothing to renew.

Does DebtFree do the debt snowball?

Yes. It does the debt snowball, the debt avalanche, your own order, and an Optimized order that tests orders for the lowest total interest. You can compare them on your own numbers.

Can I use DebtFree on iPhone?

Yes, in Safari. Add it to the Home Screen and it opens like an app and works offline. It is not in the App Store yet.
