How they differ

  • Sign-up. Undebt.it saves and tracks a plan in a free account, created with an email or a Google login. DebtFree has no accounts: open it and start typing.
  • Where your numbers live. Undebt.it keeps your plan in your account on its service. DebtFree keeps everything on your phone or computer, with an optional backup to your own Google Drive.
  • Bank access. Undebt.it+ offers bank sync as an option. DebtFree never connects to a bank; you type your own balances and payments, and autopay payments are logged for you on their due day.
  • Phones. Both run in a browser and can be added to a phone’s home screen. DebtFree is also on Google Play as an Android app.
  • Price. Undebt.it’s core is free and Undebt.it+ is $10 a year. DebtFree’s core is free and Premium is a single payment of $24, with no renewal.

Where Undebt.it is stronger

Undebt.it offers eight payoff methods to DebtFree’s four, has years of history behind it, and its paid tier costs less in its first two years than DebtFree Premium. If automatic balance updates from your bank matter most, its optional bank sync does something DebtFree deliberately does not.

What DebtFree adds

  • Optimized order: tests payoff orders and uses the one with the lowest total interest, next to snowball, avalanche and your own order.
  • 0% promos that end on time: promo and balance-transfer rates switch to the regular rate on the end date, so the plan shows what happens after.
  • Logging in one tap: “Paid as planned” for the month, autopay logged for you, and reminders before each due date.
  • Fill from a statement: pick screenshots of a card statement and the phone reads the balance, rate and minimum on the device. The images never leave it.

Questions people ask

Is DebtFree free like Undebt.it?

Yes. The planner, all four payoff orders, payment tracking and reminders are free with unlimited debts. Premium adds What-If tools, bills, sinking funds and amortization export for a one-time $24.

Does DebtFree need an email address?

No. There is no account, no email and no password. Your data stays on your device.

Can DebtFree sync with my bank like Undebt.it+?

No, by design. DebtFree never asks for a bank login. Autopay payments are logged for you, and you can update a balance from your statement whenever you like.

Will my numbers match Undebt.it’s?

They should be close for the same order and the same monthly amount. Small differences come from how each tool rounds and when it applies interest within a month.
