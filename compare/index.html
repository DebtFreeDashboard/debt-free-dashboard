The short version

  • DebtFree needs no account and never asks for a bank login. Your numbers stay on your device, the core planner is free, and Premium is one payment of $24.
  • Undebt.it is a long-running web planner with eight payoff methods. The core is free with an account; Undebt.it+ adds bank sync and more tools for $10 a year.
  • Debt Payoff Planner is a phone app on both stores with a large user base. The free version shows ads; Pro is a subscription.

What all three do

Each one takes your balances, interest rates and minimum payments, adds the extra you can put toward debt each month, and shows a payoff order and a debt-free date. Each one does the debt snowball (smallest balance first) and the debt avalanche (highest rate first), and lets you set your own order. The differences are in price, sign-up, bank access and where your numbers are kept.

Where each one is strongest

  • Undebt.it: the most payoff methods of the three, a very low yearly price for its paid tier, and optional bank sync for people who want balances to update on their own.
  • Debt Payoff Planner: native apps on both the App Store and Google Play, and an optional account that carries a plan across devices.
  • DebtFree: nothing to sign up for and no bank login, an Optimized order that tests payoff orders for the lowest total interest, autopay payments logged for you, and a one-time price instead of a subscription.

Questions people ask

Which debt payoff app is free?

All three have a free version. DebtFree’s planner, tracking and reminders are free with unlimited debts, and Premium is a one-time $24. Undebt.it’s core planner is free with an account, and Undebt.it+ is $10 a year. Debt Payoff Planner is free with ads, and Pro is a subscription.

Which debt payoff apps work without linking a bank account?

DebtFree never asks for a bank login. Debt Payoff Planner has you type in payments. Undebt.it works without one, and offers bank sync as an option in its paid tier.

Do I need to create an account?

Not for DebtFree, which has no accounts at all. Debt Payoff Planner’s account is optional. Undebt.it uses a free account to save and track a plan.

Is there a DebtFree app for iPhone?

Not in the App Store yet. On iPhone, DebtFree runs in Safari and can be added to the Home Screen, where it opens like an app and works offline. On Android it is on Google Play.
