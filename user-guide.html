Step 01
Get the app

Same app everywhere, no account. Your numbers are saved on the device you use, so pick the one you'll check each month.

A

Android

Install DebtFree: Debt Payoff Tracker from Google Play.

B

iPhone and iPad

Open mydebtdashboard.com/app/dashboard.html in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new icon from then on: phone notifications and app lock only work in the Home Screen app.

C

Computer

Open the same address in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Chrome and Edge offer an Install button in the address bar.

💡Just looking? The first screen is an example plan with made-up debts. Explore it, then tap Start with my numbers to clear it and add your own.
💳
Step 02
Add your debts

Have your latest statements handy. You can add every debt in one go, and add more at any time.

1

Tap + and choose Add a debt

On a phone, the + sits in the middle of the bottom bar. On a computer, the Add a Debt card is on the Home tab.

2

Balance, APR and minimum payment

That's all the plan needs. Enter APR as a number (22.99, not 0.2299). The name is optional, and a minimum left blank is estimated and marked (est.) until you change it.

3

More details, if you want them

Open More details for type, lender, due day (and a second day if you pay twice a month), credit limit and 0% promo. Typing a name like "Car loan" or picking a lender sets the type for you. The due day is what powers This Month, the calendar and reminders.

4

Save & add another debt

Saves this one and clears the form for the next. A note at the top shows how many you've added and your debt-free date so far.

5

Save & see my plan

With one debt left to save, you'll see a preview first. With an empty form, it takes you straight to your plan.

6

Your plan is ready (first time only)

Slide to set how much goes to debt each month and watch the debt-free date move, add due days if you like, then save, or save and turn on reminders in the same tap. You can change the amount later under Strategy › Your Monthly Plan.

Step 03
Home and This Month

Home answers two questions: where you stand, and what's left to pay this month.

💰

Total Owed

Everything you owe, the share already paid down, and a split by account.

🔥

Interest/mo, Freedom Date, Interest Saved

This month's interest, the month your last debt reaches $0 on your plan, and the interest your plan avoids compared with minimums only.

📅

This Month

Every payment your plan has for this month. The ring fills as you log them. Switch between List and Calendar at the top of the card.

🩺

Plan check

Shows up only when it applies: a 0% promo ending within 60 days, a planned payment smaller than this month's interest, or a card at 90% of its limit.

🎯Where your extra goes: the card under This Month names the debt your plan is focused on and what it sends there each month.
✅
Step 04
Log payments

Logging keeps balances, the ring and your debt-free date accurate. Four ways, fastest first.

1

On autopay: nothing to tap

Open a debt (Edit), tick On autopay and save. From then on its payment logs itself on each due day, and the app tells you when it did, with Undo. Leave the amount blank to use the minimum, or type what your autopay sends. Anything your plan sends beyond that still shows in This Month. Needs a due day.

2

Paid as planned (the whole month)

Tap Paid as planned in This Month, or + then Paid as planned. Untick anything you didn't pay or change an amount, then log them all at once. One Undo reverses it.

3

Swipe one payment

In This Month, swipe a payment right to log the planned amount, or tap Log. Swipe left (or tap ⋯) for Later: schedule it for another date or skip it this month.

4

Any amount, any debt

Tap + then Log a payment. Pick the debt, the amount you actually paid (minimum plus any extra) and the date. A future date is saved as pending and counts on that day.

👆

“Did these go out?”

When a payment’s day comes, the app asks once, the next time you open it. Tap Paid for each one (change the amount first if it was different), Skip this month, or It’s on autopay so it logs itself from then on. Due-day notifications open the same check; on Android they have a Paid button too.

🧾

Statement check-in

When a statement arrives, open the payment's ⋯ menu and enter the statement balance and date. The app records the difference (interest, fees) on that date, so the balance matches your lender's without a bank login.

↩️

Fix a mistake

Every payment is in the Payment Log on Home. Edit or delete any entry, even an old one, and balances are recalculated from the start.

🔔
Step 05
Payment reminders

Free. Open them from Get payment reminders at the bottom of the This Month card. Your debts need a due day first.

1

Phone notifications

Choose before each payment (1, 3 or 7 days ahead, on the day, plus a check-in on the 1st), every Monday or on the 1st of the month, then tap Turn on notifications and allow them when your phone asks. They arrive around 9 AM your time.

2

Or calendar reminders

Adds a repeating event for each due date to your phone's calendar, which does the reminding. On Android you get one Google Calendar link per date; on iPhone each opens in the Calendar app.

🔒What leaves your phone: for notifications, our reminder service stores the phone's push address, your due days as numbers, how often to remind and your time zone. No names, balances or amounts. The notification text is written on your phone, and account names only appear if you tick Show account names in reminders. Calendar reminders send nothing to us at all.
⚔️
Step 06
Strategy

The Strategy tab sets the order your extra money goes to. Each card shows that order's debt-free month and total interest on your own numbers.

①

Optimized

Tests every payoff order and uses the one with the lowest total interest, including what happens when 0% promos end.

②

Avalanche

Highest APR first. A 0% promo card is ranked by the rate it will switch to.

③

Snowball

Smallest balance first, so accounts close sooner.

④

Your own order

Drag debts into any order you like.

📊Your Monthly Plan: set the extra amount you put toward debt each month above the minimums. When a debt is paid off, its minimum rolls into the next one automatically. Premium adds the payoff-by-payoff roadmap, amortization schedules and Dial-a-Date (pick a date, see the monthly amount it takes).
🔮
Step 07
What If

A sandbox. Nothing you try here changes your real plan.

📅

Additional monthly payment

Slide to see time saved, interest saved and the new Freedom Date, measured against your current plan.

💵

Lump sum (Premium)

Model a one-time payment such as a tax refund or bonus, on top of any lump sums you've already planned.

⚠️0 months saved? If planned lump sums already pay everything off, the monthly slider can show 0 months saved while still showing interest saved. That's correct.
🏆
Step 08
Wins

Paying off debt takes a while. Wins keeps the progress visible.

🥇

Badges and streaks

Unlock automatically from your logged payments and balances. Up Next shows the closest ones and how far away they are.

📅

Monthly recap and year in review

The first time you open the app in a new month, you'll see last month: paid toward debt, how much less you owe, debts paid off, badges, and whether the debt-free date moved. From mid-December there's a year in review. Both are on the Wins tab, each with a card to share (no dollar amounts on the card).

✅

Paid-off debts

Every debt you clear stays here with what it took. A shareable card appears when it happens; share it or tap Done.

💾
Step 09
Privacy, app lock and backup

Your debts are stored on your device, not on our servers. That makes backups your job, and the tools below make it quick. All of them are in Settings (the gear, top right).

🔒

App lock

Set a PIN, and optionally Face ID or fingerprint. Choose whether it locks right away or after 1 or 5 minutes away. It's a screen lock, not encryption. Forgot the PIN? The lock can be removed after a 60-second wait, so you're never locked out of your own data.

☁️

Back up to Google Drive

Saves a copy to your own Google Drive. Use it to move to a new phone or recover after clearing your browser.

📤

Export and Import Backup

Export downloads one file with all debts, payments and settings. Import restores it on any device.

🔄

Check for Updates

Updates arrive on their own. If you want the newest version right away, tap this; your data is kept.

⚡
Step 10
Troubleshooting
🔕

No notifications

On iPhone, open the app from the Home Screen icon, not Safari. Check the phone's settings allow notifications for DebtFree (or Chrome on Android), and that your debts have due days.

🫥

My debts disappeared

Each browser and the Home Screen app keep separate data. Open the one you used before, or restore from Google Drive or an exported backup.

🧮

Balance doesn't match my statement

Use statement check-in on that debt. Interest and fees between payments are why balances drift.

🎯

Transfer fee looks wrong

The fee is fixed when you add the debt. If an older transfer shows the wrong %, open Edit and type the right rate once: the label is corrected and the dollars you were charged stay the same.

📆

A payment shows as pending

It has a future date. It counts on that date automatically.

⭐

Premium on a new device

Website: enter your license key in Settings. Play app: Premium is restored from your Google Play account.
