04 60-Day Affiliate Launch Timeline

Week 1
Enable Gumroad affiliate program at 30%. Set cookie to 30 days. Create a short landing page or PDF one-pager about the affiliate program.
Week 1
Build your target list: 30 creators in the debt-free/personal finance niche with 5K–150K followers. Use TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube search — "#debtfree", "#debtpayoff", "#avalanchemethod".
Week 1
Send first 10 outreach emails. Personalize each one with a reference to their specific content. Use Template #1 or #2 above.
Week 2
Send follow-up #1 to anyone who opened but didn't reply. Send next 10 cold outreach emails from your target list.
Week 2
Onboard any affiliates who said yes — send Template #5 within 24 hours. Attach the dashboard file. Ask when they plan to post.
Week 3
Send follow-up #2 (breakup email) to Week 1 non-responders. Send next 10 cold emails. Start podcast outreach with Template #6.
Week 4
Check in with active affiliates — ask if they need content ideas or screenshots. Reshare any posts they make on your own channels.
Month 2
Review affiliate performance in Gumroad. Double down on affiliates who've generated sales — offer them bonus commission (35%) for next 30 days.
Month 2
Run a 48-hour promotion (10% off) and notify all affiliates in advance. Give them graphics and caption templates to post during the sale.
Month 2
Target: 5 active affiliates generating 3+ sales each = 15+ affiliate sales/month = additional $494/month net on top of your direct sales.
